Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Email hello@lamsa.app

How does Lamsa work?

Upload one or more photos of your room, choose a room type, style and budget, and Lamsa's AI generates a full redesign — keeping your room's actual layout (walls, doors, windows) and only changing furniture, colors and decor.

How many free credits do I get?

Every new account gets 1 free design to try. After that, a Weekly or Monthly plan gives you a refilling credit balance — each generation (AI Interior Design, Quick Rearrange, or a Style Gallery look) costs 1 credit.

What's the difference between the Weekly and Monthly plans?

Weekly gives 10 credits for €5.99, billed every week. Monthly gives 40 credits for €14.99, billed every month — better value if you design often. Both renew automatically until canceled.

Can I cancel or change my subscription?

Yes — email hello@lamsa.app with your account email and we'll take care of it.

Is my room photo stored or shared?

Your photo is sent to our AI provider only to generate your design, and the result is saved to your account so you can revisit it. We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

What languages does Lamsa support?

Lamsa's interface is available in 8 languages, including Arabic and English.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, login details and remaining credits — it can't be undone.

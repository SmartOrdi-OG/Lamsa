About Lamsa

Made by SmartOrdi

Lamsa is an AI-powered interior design app. Upload a photo of any room and Lamsa redesigns it — same walls, doors and windows, new furniture, colors and decor — in seconds.

It's built for anyone who wants to see what their space could look like before spending a single riyal — whether you're furnishing a new home, refreshing an old one, or just curious.

What You Can Do

AI Interior Design Upload a room photo, pick a style and budget, get a full redesign.
Quick Rearrange Rearrange your existing furniture in the same room, no new pieces.
3D Designer Build a room from scratch — walls, finishes and furniture — in a live 3D preview.
Style Gallery Browse ready-made styles and apply one directly to a photo of your own room.
